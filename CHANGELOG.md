# Changelog

## 0.1

Initial release.

- `ooxml/zip`: `zip->hash`, `zip->entries`, `zip-write!`, `zip-entries->bytes`, `deflate`, `crc32`.
- `ooxml/rels`: `rel`, `parse-rels`, `rels-xml`, `rels-name`, `owner-of-rels`, `resolve-target`, `next-rid`.
- `ooxml/package`: `read-package`, `write-package`, `package-reachable`, `prune-package`, content-type parsing and rebuilding, `check-package`.
